# CapCut Master Plan: EP05 Production Manifest

## Video Title: Future Desk OS Editorial
## Leading Question: If Amazon's strategic retreat from proprietary flagship AI models signals that the marginal ROI of building foundational models fails to justify its massive compute and CapEx overhead, are enterprise leaders over-capitalizing internal decision systems by trying to own the intelligence stack rather than architecting model-agnostic operational workflows that protect long-term margins?

### 1. TIMELINE STRUCTURE
**Track 1 (Video):**
- Cut every 2.5 seconds.
- Start with the Kling AI Clip (Server Rack).
- Transitions: Use "Speed Ramp (Hero/Bullet Time Curve)" to smooth out AI clip start/end delays.

**Track 2 (Voiceover):**
- Staging: Alternate Host 1 and Host 2 dialogue.
- Gaps: Max 0.2 seconds gap between dialogue blocks to maintain flow.

**Track 3 (Layer 1 - Atmo):**
- Content: Subtle Industrial Hum / Server Room Ambience.
- Volume: -24 dB (to mask dry AI voice acoustics).

**Track 4 (Layer 2 & 3 - Foley/Accents):**
- Layer 2 (Foley): Subtle "Whoosh" sound effects at every perspective change (e.g., Host 1 to Host 2).
- Layer 3 (Impact): Low-End "Boom" sound effect (-12 dB) at the opening question from Host 2.

### 2. DYNAMIC CAPTIONS PROTOCOL (Retention-Fit)
- **Font:** Montserrat Bold or Inter Black (Uppercase).
- **Alignment:** Centered in the lower third of the screen (safe zone for TikTok/Reel UI-buttons).
- **Animation:** Word-by-word highlighting (Active Word Highlight).
- **Color Code:**
  - Standard Text: White (`#FFFFFF`)
  - Active Word: Executive-Yellow (`#FFD700`)
  - Financial Terms (CapEx, ROI, Margin, $50M, etc.): Alert-Red (`#FF3333`)
