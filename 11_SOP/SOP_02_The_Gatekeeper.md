# SOP 02: The Gatekeeper

## Purpose
The Gatekeeper (`gatekeeper.mjs`) is the second stage of the Future Desk OS data processing pipeline. Its function is to apply a qualitative and quantitative scoring model to the raw signals ingested by the Sensor Engine, ensuring that only the most relevant and impactful information proceeds to the next stage.

## Core Protocols

### 1. Zero-Cost Tooling Protocol
To maintain a zero-budget-bootstrap approach until at least Day 31, all production processes must adhere to the following:
- **AI Models:** Utilize free tiers and available credits exclusively. This includes, but is not limited to, Kling AI (Free Credits), Minimax Web (Free Tier), and any other relevant services.
- **Software:** All software used for production (e.g., video editing, content creation) must be the free version (e.g., CapCut Desktop Free Version).
- **No Paid Subscriptions:** Do not integrate any tool or service that requires a paid subscription beyond the already active CLI Pro-Subscription.

### 2. Language Protocol
To align with our international focus (`target_market: "North_America_Global"`), all data operations must be conducted exclusively in English (en-US).
- **Data:** All stored data, including headlines, summaries, and notes, must be in English.
- **Prompts:** All prompts sent to the CLI-LLM for scoring or any other purpose must be written in clear, concise, American English.
- **Outputs:** All outputs generated by any part of the system, whether for internal analysis or external publication, must be in English.

## Scoring and Rejection Gate
The Gatekeeper uses a 25-point scoring system executed by the CLI-LLM. Each signal is evaluated from the perspective of a North American B2B decision-maker (CEO, CTO, Ops Director) across five criteria:

- `surprise_score` (1-5)
- `scale_score` (1-5)
- `timeliness_score` (1-5)
- `longevity_score` (1-5)
- `actionability_score` (1-5)

**The Rejection Gate is absolute:**
- **`total_editorial_score >= 18`**: The signal is deemed valuable and its status is set to `"approved"`.
- **`total_editorial_score < 18`**: The signal is deemed not valuable enough for further processing and its status is set to `"rejected"`.

Only `"approved"` signals are saved to `02_Signals/scored_signals.json` for further analysis.
