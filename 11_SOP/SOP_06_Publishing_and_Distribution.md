# SOP 06: Publishing and Distribution

## Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) outlines the strategy and execution for the Publishing Department within the Future Desk OS. It details the 1-to-10 Asset-Multiplier-Rule, ensuring that a single approved signal is transformed into a comprehensive suite of platform-native content assets for the North American B2B market, while strictly adhering to the Zero-Budget Protocol.

## Core Directives

### 1. The 1-to-10 Asset-Multiplier-Rule
For every single approved and editorially synthesized signal, the Publishing Department must generate a minimum of 10 distinct, platform-native content assets. This ensures maximum reach and impact across our target distribution channels.
-   **Until Day 31 (Product-Market-Fit Phase):** All content assets are to be produced "on stock" using this 1-to-10 multiplier process.
-   **No Manual Content Creation:** During this phase, no content may be manually written from scratch. All assets must be programmatically generated via the LLM interface, ensuring scalability and adherence to the Zero-Budget Protocol.

### 2. Platform-Native Content Transformation
Each generated asset must be tailored to the specific requirements and best practices of its target platform:

-   **YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels Metadata:**
    *   **Titles:** 3 alternative, high-contrast scroll-stop titles (max. 50 characters).
    *   **Description:** Algorithm-optimized video description including 3 precise B2B hashtags (e.g., #SupplyChain, #EnterpriseAI, #FirstPrinciples).
-   **LinkedIn Executive Briefing:**
    *   **Format:** Professional, data-enriched post for CEOs, COOs, CFOs.
    *   **Structure:** Hard counter-intuitive hook, short analysis of second-order effects, clear derivation of the net ROI problem, and an open closing question to foster discussion in the comments.
-   **X (Twitter) Thread:**
    *   **Format:** 5-part thread.
    *   **Content:** Breaks down the causality cascade from technology to operational decision quickly and concisely.
-   **Newsletter Lead Section ("The Future Desk Brief"):**
    *   **Format:** Editorially crafted article component (approx. 250 words).
    *   **Focus:** Why this trend will change companies' operational net margins in the next quarter.

### 3. Robust Sanitization Protocol
The proven JSON sanitizer (Trailing Comma Fix & Regex cleaning) must be applied to all LLM responses to prevent parsing errors and ensure data integrity.

## Financial Protocol
All incurred LLM token costs for content generation must be logged strictly in **Net EUR/USD** (without VAT).

By adhering to this SOP, the Publishing Department will efficiently and effectively disseminate our insights, establishing Future Desk OS as a leading voice in the North American B2B market.
