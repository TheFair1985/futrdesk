# SOP 03: Editorial Engine

## Purpose
The Editorial Engine (`editorial_engine.mjs`) is the core of our content synthesis process. It transforms raw, scored signals into actionable, engaging narrative structures for our North American B2B audience. This SOP ensures consistency, quality, and adherence to our strategic communication directives.

## Core Directives

### 1. Language & Market Focus
All editorial output, including questions, scripts, and metadata, must be exclusively in **English (US)** and tailored for the **North American B2B market**. This includes tone, terminology, and relevance.

### 2. The 1-Question Rule
Every content piece must be distilled into **exactly one (1) deep, counter-intuitive leading question**. This question serves as the central thesis, designed to provoke thought and engagement from our executive audience. It must connect seemingly disparate signals into a causal narrative.

### 3. The 2-Host Dynamic Clash
All video scripts must feature a dynamic dialogue between two fixed archetypes:
-   **HOST 1 (The Analyst / You):** Calm, authoritative, analytical. Explains causalities, second-order effects, and financial realities using hard mental models (First Principles, net margins, capex friction).
-   **HOST 2 (The Challenger / AI Partner):** Represents the skeptical North American B2B audience (CEO/COO). Interrupts sharply, asks tough questions ("Why should a CFO care?", "Where is the net ROI?", "What happens when this breaks in month 6?").
This dynamic clash is essential for demonstrating critical thinking and addressing audience concerns directly.

### 4. Visual Metadata Cues
Each dialogue block in the script must be accompanied by a technical visual instruction (Visual Cue). These cues are critical for our downstream Kling AI / CapCut video production pipeline. They must be descriptive, actionable, and avoid generic instructions.
*Example:* `[Visual Cue: 35mm lens, handheld camera push-in on an industrial robotics warehouse, natural lighting, no CGI]`

### 5. Human Co-Founder Approval Gate
**No script shall proceed to video production (Episode 04) without explicit approval from the human Co-Founder.** This is a mandatory quality gate to ensure strategic alignment, narrative integrity, and adherence to brand voice. The 2-Host Dynamic Clash must be evident and effective in the final script.

## Financial Protocol
All computational costs associated with script synthesis (e.g., LLM calls) must be calculated and logged exclusively in **Net EUR/USD** (without VAT).

By adhering to this SOP, we ensure that our editorial output is not only insightful but also strategically aligned and production-ready.
